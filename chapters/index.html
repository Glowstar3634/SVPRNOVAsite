University Chapters are SVPRNOVA's formally organized collegiate communities: student-led groups that can originate research locally while participating in a wider inter-university research ecosystem.

RESEARCH HUBS

Research opportunity
should not depend on
where you study.

Research Hubs extend SVPRNOVA beyond universities. They provide a local identity for researchers organized through schools, districts, libraries, community STEM organizations, and other communities while keeping them connected to the wider SVPRNOVA network.

The structure is intentionally broader than a university chapter: the local home can change, while access to collaborators, projects, knowledge, and research opportunity remains network-wide.

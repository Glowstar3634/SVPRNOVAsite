RESEARCH HUBS

Research opportunity
should not depend on
where you study.

Research Hubs extend SVPRNOVA beyond universities. They provide a local identity for researchers organized through schools, districts, libraries, community STEM organizations, youth groups, and other communities while keeping them connected to the wider SVPRNOVA network.

The local home can change, while access to collaborators, projects, knowledge, and research opportunity remains network-wide.

THE SVPR RESEARCH MODEL

From questionto consequence.

Research is not a straight line. We begin with a question, trace the stars of existing human knowledge, connect what the problem requires, and follow the consequences of what we find.

01 / QUESTION

What do we want to understand?

Students identify questions worth pursuing—not because they were assigned, but because the answer may reveal something meaningful.

02 / TRACE

Navigate what humanity already knows.

Trace through literature, mathematical frameworks, datasets, known technologies, experiments, software, accessible resources, and people across the research network.

PAPERSTHEORYDATATOOLSPEOPLE
03 / CONNECT

Follow the question across disciplines.

A mathematical idea may require simulation. Simulation may reveal behavior. Behavior generates data. Data may suggest a system to build. The work becomes interdisciplinary because reality is interdisciplinary.

04 / CONSEQUENCE

An answer is not the only thing we find.

In reaching an answer to the original question, researchers may uncover methods, insights, applications, and entirely new questions along the way.
